Our Story

Welcome to Butter and Bloom Bakery! With a love of baking in the heart of Helsinki, we create handmade, organic baked goods using traditional fermentation techniques and local Nordic ingredients.

At our bakery, we are proud of the `Daily` freshness of all our products. You will find that all our products are baked as early as possible every single morning, from sourdough loaves to croissants, all to warm up your day and make every coffee break a delightful moment.
